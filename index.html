Questions

Why does a video say "no captions"?

The creator has turned captions off and YouTube has not generated automatic ones. Try another video.

Does it work with Shorts and live streams?

Yes. Shorts, live replays, embed links and mobile links are all supported.

Can I translate the transcript?

Yes. Choose a language in the translate menu and the transcript reloads translated.

Is it free?

Yes, every tool is free with no account.
